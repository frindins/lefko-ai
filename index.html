FAQ

What counts as a message?

One chat reply. Generations have their own daily counts — but they share the same daily budget, so a video-and-images day leaves less room for messages.

Do credits expire?

Never. Packs stack, balances persist, and there's no monthly reset on credits — buy when you need video, use it whenever.

What does HD video cost?

720p is 3 credits per second, 1080p is 6 credits per second. Every paid plan also includes a free 480p · 4s Studio video daily.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime — cancelling stops the next renewal. The month you've already paid runs to its end and isn't refundable (so cancelling right after paying works like a single month with no auto-renew).

Is my data private?

The server is self-hosted on Aaron's own machine. Chats stay on that box, and the AI providers used are configured for zero data retention.

What do I need to run it?

Just a modern browser. For voice, allow the mic when your browser asks and pick a voice you like in Settings → Audio.
